UDG N-TERMINAL TAIL CHARGE ANALYSIS
===================================

Purpose
-------
Characterize how positive and negative residues are distributed along the UDG
N-terminal tail and reproduce the residue-charge, NCPR, FCR, and Das-Pappu
plots.

Input sequence
--------------
Save the following as UDG_tail.fasta:

>pdb:A
MIGQKTLYSFFSPSPARKRHAPSPEPAVQGTGVAGVPEESGDAAAIPAKKAPAGQEEPGTPPSSPLS

Part A: EMBOSS Charge
---------------------
1. Open the EMBOSS Charge web tool.
2. Paste or upload UDG_tail.fasta.
3. Set the sliding-window length to 5.
4. Run the calculation and save both the numerical table and plot.


Part B: CIDER
-------------
1. Open the CIDER web server.
2. Submit the same FASTA sequence without changing it.
3. Generate and save:
   - NCPR (net charge per residue) plot
   - FCR (fraction of charged residues) plot
   - Das-Pappu diagram of states


Interpretation of the outputs
-----------------------------
- NCPR reports the local balance of positive and negative charge.
- FCR reports the local density of charged residues irrespective of sign.
- The Das-Pappu plot classifies the sequence according to its fractions of
  positively and negatively charged residues.
