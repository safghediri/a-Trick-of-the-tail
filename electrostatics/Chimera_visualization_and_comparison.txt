ELECTROSTATIC MAP VISUALIZATION IN UCSF CHIMERA
================================================

Purpose
-------
Display APBS and DelPhi electrostatic potential maps consistently and compare

Required files
--------------
- The matching UDG-tail molecular structure
- APBS OpenDX maps
- DelPhi electrostatic potential maps

Procedure
---------
1. Open the molecular structure in UCSF Chimera.
2. Open its matching electrostatic map in Volume Viewer.
3. Set the Volume Viewer step size to 1.
4. Display the potential using the same range for every comparison:
      negative threshold: -10
      positive threshold: +10
5. Apply a consistent negative-to-positive color convention to all panels
   (for example, red for negative and blue for positive).
